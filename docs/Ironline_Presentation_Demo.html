Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Gym Owners

How does member photo ID help our gym? +
When a member is registered, front desk staff can upload or take their picture. Whenever a member checks in, their picture displays on the screen, preventing members from letting unauthorized friends use their membership pass.
What do members see inside their branded mobile app? +
Members install an app with your gym's official name and logo on their home screen. Inside, they can log their daily meals and calories, record their weekly body weight and measurements, follow custom workout routines assigned by your trainers, and track their workout streaks.
How does the streak retention system prevent member dropouts? +
Every time a member visits your gym, their streak increments (+1 day) and unlocks milestone badges (7-Day Bronze, 30-Day Silver, 100-Day Gold, 365-Day Legend). Furthermore, the built-in 1-day-per-week rest day protection ensures that taking Sunday off doesn't reset their streak — keeping them motivated and renewing every month!
How easy is it to record cash and bank fee payments? +
Extremely easy! In the Fees tab, click "Record Payment", select Cash, Bank Transfer, or Mobile Wallet, enter the amount, and click Save. The member's status updates to Paid immediately, their renewal date advances by 30 days, and you can print a clean receipt.
What if our gym basement has no internet or cellular reception? +
Ironline is built offline-first! Even with zero mobile data, check-ins are recorded safely on the device. As soon as the phone catches WiFi or cell service, visits automatically synchronize with your gym dashboard with the exact timestamp.
