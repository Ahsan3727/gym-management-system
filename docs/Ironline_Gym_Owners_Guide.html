01

5-Minute Quick-Start Guide

Get your gym operations digitized in four simple steps without installing hardware or purchasing specialized scanner guns:

1
Configure Your Gym Profile
Navigate to Gym Profile in the sidebar. Upload your gym emblem/logo, address, phone number, and operating hours.
2
Print Your Reception Desk QR Sign
Click "Print Reception Sign". Place this crisp sign on your counter. Tokens automatically roll over at midnight.
3
Register Your Members
Go to Customers -> Click "+ Add Customer". Set their name, username, phone, and monthly membership fee.
4
Distribute Your Branded App
Have members scan your desk QR sign. Their phone prompts them to install your branded app directly on their home screen.

03

Smart Reception Check-In & Offline Resilience

Zero-Click Scan

Native Phone Camera

Members scan your front desk QR code using their standard iPhone Camera or Android Google Lens. If logged in, the app instantly records check-in with zero clicks.

100% Offline

Basement Dead-Zone Safe

No internet? No problem. The check-in queues in the member's phone storage with an accurate timestamp and syncs automatically the moment WiFi or mobile data connects.

Daily Rollover

Midnight Date Expiry

Tokens expire at 23:59:59 (midnight) each day, eliminating rolling 24h timer confusion and ensuring check-ins match gym calendar days.

💡
Backup Passcode on Printable Desk Sign
If a member's phone camera is scratched or unavailable, your printed desk sign includes today's 6-character daily backup code. Members or receptionists can enter this code for instant manual validation.
04

Monthly Fee & Revenue Management (PKR)

Designed specifically for cash, bank transfer, and mobile wallet payments common in private gyms:

Fee Status Meaning System Action
🟢 Paid Monthly membership fee is fully settled. Membership valid through renewal date. Full gym access granted.
🟡 Due Soon Fee expires within the next 5 days. Soft reminder displayed on member's dashboard to pay dues.
🔴 Overdue Due date has passed without payment recorded. Flagged on front desk attendance log and customer list for collection.
⚪ Unpaid New invoice generated for upcoming cycle. Awaiting receipt of cash, card, or bank transfer confirmation.
Time Saver

1-Click Bulk Monthly Invoicing

At the beginning of each billing month, click "Bulk Generate Invoices" under Fees. The system automatically creates invoices for all active members based on their individual monthly rate.

Multi-Channel

Flexible Payment Methods

Record payments via Cash, Bank Transfer, POS Card Swipe, or Mobile Wallets (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Raast). Attach reference numbers and issue clean, printable receipts.

📋 Front-Desk Staff Quick-Reference Cheatsheet

Keep this quick reference guide handy for receptionists and front-desk staff:

Member Situation Recommended Front Desk Action
Member forgot phone at home Open Attendance tab -> Search member name -> Click "Check In".
Member's phone has no internet Have them scan the desk QR as usual! The app queues their visit locally and syncs automatically when online.
Member's phone camera is cracked Member taps "Enter Passcode" in app and inputs today's 6-character code printed on the desk sign.
Member wants to pay monthly fee Open Fees -> Click "Record Payment" -> Choose Cash/Bank/Card -> Enter PKR amount -> Save.
Member forgot their password Open Customers -> Search member -> Click "Reset Password" to provide temporary login.
